About TechOps

We make sure ACM FAST's events, competitions and platforms are ready before the day, steady while it's happening, and packed away neatly afterwards.

TechOps looks after the technical side of ACM FAST: the infrastructure, the software and the hands-on work at events. We work alongside the other ACM departments, never in place of them.

Whether it's an introductory session, a contest or a workshop, we get the hardware ready, keep everything running while it's live, and clean up after. That way speakers, organisers and participants can just enjoy the day.

An event day with TechOps

Before the doors open

Everything is ready before anyone walks in.

  • Labs and workstations checked
  • Network and contest environment set up
  • Screens and sound tested

While it is live

We stay on the floor so the day never pauses.

  • Standing by with participants
  • Watching the network and servers
  • Fixing problems on the spot

When it is over

We leave things as we found them.

  • Equipment packed up safely
  • Labs restored to normal
  • Everything handed back tidy

Who we are

TechOps is the engineering and operational backbone of ACM NUCES Karachi, whose motto is “Where Resilience Meets Innovation”. We build platforms, support competitive programming events and help students grow as technologists.

What ACM hosts

ACM NUCES Karachi runs signature computing events like Coders' Cup and Developers' Day, testing students on algorithmic speed, problem-solving, product ideas and technical resilience.

Who we work with

We team up with the Technology, Computing, Event Management, Automation, Marketing and Media wings, and with partners like Raqami Islamic Digital Bank, Teknofest Pakistan 4.0 and AI Season.
